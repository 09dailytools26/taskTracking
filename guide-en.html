1Publish the app (once, on a computer)

The app is published with GitHub Pages. Create a separate repository from the homework notebook app.

  1. Unzip study-planner.zip.It contains 8 files: index.html, guide.html, guide-en.html, sw.js, manifest.webmanifest and 3 icon images.
  2. On GitHub, click ＋ → "New repository". Name it study-planner, choose Public, and create it.
  3. Click "uploading an existing file", drag all 8 files in, and click "Commit changes".Drag the files inside the folder, not the folder itself.
  4. Go to "Settings" → "Pages". Set Branch to main and the folder to / (root), then click "Save".
  5. After a few minutes, the Pages screen shows https://your-name.github.io/study-planner/. Send this address to the person who will use the app.
Only the empty app is published. Your subjects, tasks and study time are saved only on your own iPhone.

2Add it to your iPhone Home Screen

  1. Open the address in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button → "Add to Home Screen".If you see "Open as Web App", turn it on.
  3. Tap "Add". A "Study" icon appears on your Home Screen.
  4. Close Safari and open the app from the icon.
Don't start using it in Safari.
Safari and the Home Screen icon keep separate data.

The first screen lets you choose the language (日本語 / English) and start with examples or start empty. Examples are marked "(ex.)" so you can edit or delete them later. You can change the language anytime in Settings.

3Set up subjects and colors

  1. Tap Settings → "Subjects & colors".
  2. Tap ＋ Add subject, type a name and pick one of 12 pastel colors."Pick any color" lets you choose any color you like.
  3. Tap a subject to rename it, change its color or delete it.

4Add weekly tasks

For tasks that repeat every week, like vocabulary or worksheets. Go to Settings → "Weekly tasks" → ＋ Add weekly task.

Set daysChoose the days you do it (all days for every day). It shows under "To do today" on those days.
Set deadlineNo fixed day, just "by this weekday every week". It shows under "Due this week" from the start of the week, and disappears for that week once done.
Off during testsWhen on, the task is hidden from a few days before a test until its last day, so you can focus on test prep. Change how many days in Settings → "Test period".

5The Today screen

TopDays left until your next test. Tap it to open that test.
Study timeToday's total study time and time per subject.
To do todayGrouped into "Daily tasks", "Due this week" and "Due soon (within 7 days)". Tap the circle to mark it done.
DoneWhat you finished today. Tap "Undo" if you made a mistake.

Tap ⋯ to skip a weekly task for today, or to edit or delete an assignment. Use ‹ › at the top to see other days.

6Assignments with due dates

For things due on a specific date, like "lab report due Oct 3". Add them with ＋ Add task on the Today or Plan screen.

  1. Choose a subject, then enter what to do and the due date.
  2. It appears under "To do today" from 7 days before it's due. See all assignments under "Assignments" in Plan.
  3. It shows "Due tomorrow" the day before, "Due today" on the day, and "Overdue" after.

7Tests and study plans

Add a test

  1. Tap Plan → ＋ Add test.
  2. Enter the test name, first day and last day.
  3. For each subject, enter the exam date and scope (textbook or workbook pages). Only fill in the subjects you know.

Make a study plan

The test screen lists the scope and study plan for each subject. Use ＋ Add to study plan to add items like "Workbook p.20–35" with a due date. The due date starts as the day before that subject's exam. Study plan items also show up in "To do today" as they get close.

The top of the test screen shows days left and how much of your plan is done. Tap "Edit" to update the scope.

8Log your study time

With the timerTap ▶ Start on the Today screen and choose a subject. Tap Stop when you finish to log it. The timer keeps running even if you close the app.
AfterwardsTap ＋ Log and enter the subject and minutes. The "+30m", "+1h" buttons add time. Change the date to log a past day.

9The Stats screen

Stats shows your study time for the week as a chart by day. Tap a bar to see that day by subject. Below it are the totals per subject and how many daily tasks you completed. Use ‹ › to see past weeks.

10Backup and changing phones

Your data lives only on your iPhone, so save a backup now and then. If it's been 2 weeks since the last one, a reminder shows on the Today screen.

  1. Settings → Save backup → "Save to Files" (iCloud Drive, etc.).
  2. On a new iPhone, add the app to the Home Screen, then tap Restore and choose the backup file.
Restoring replaces the data currently on that iPhone with the backup.

11FAQ

Is old data deleted automatically?
The app never deletes it. But data can be lost if you delete the Home Screen icon or clear Safari's history and website data in iPhone Settings. A backup lets you get it back.
Does it work offline?
Yes, after you've opened it once from the icon.
Can someone see my data if they know the address?
No. Your data is only on your iPhone.
What happens when the app is updated?
Upload the new files to the study-planner repository on GitHub. Your data stays. Close and reopen the app to see the new version.
